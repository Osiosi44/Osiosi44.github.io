Overview

HIFZI helps learners track memorized ayah ranges, plan Muraja’ah, and review their own recitations. Core practice works offline without an account.

Features

  • Search all 114 surahs and track memorized portions.
  • Schedule revision and record how well you remembered each portion.
  • Record and replay personal recitations.
  • Review practice history and progress over time.
