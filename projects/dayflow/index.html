Overview

DayFlow is an offline-first planner for tasks, routines, and notes. The screenshots show the app with sample data.

Features

  • Plan tasks with priorities, checklists, and reminders.
  • Browse upcoming work by week or month.
  • Create recurring tasks and step-by-step routines.
  • Write searchable notes and review completed work.
